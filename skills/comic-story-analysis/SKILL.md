# Story Analysis

Follow the [shared contract](../../references/production-contract.md). Use [paragraph completeness](../../references/paragraph-completeness.md) and [source fidelity](../../references/source-fidelity.md).

Read the full canonical chapter and keep bytes unchanged. Explicitly exclude capture noise. Decompose every clause with offsets/hashes, speaker/uncertainty and retained facts; obtain independent paragraph-by-paragraph review. One canonical analysis contains synopsis, cast/classification/introductions, beat sheet, retention and continuity/asset-state ledgers. Views reference that data instead of duplicating its entire contents.

For each beat record source anchors, setting/time, current actors, trigger, action/reaction, consequence and unresolved hook. Preserve conditions, claims versus facts, private thoughts and later-reveal boundaries. Named speakers/meaningful supporting interactions are not generic crowds; ambiguous speakers stay uncertain.

Count words and estimate narrative density, but do not choose pages from unit count. Group compatible facts into a concrete visual moment; the exhaustive audit map still covers every unit. Identify genuinely recurring/story-critical assets and new design questions for the next gate. No art or publication approval at this phase.
